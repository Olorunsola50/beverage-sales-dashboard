# beverage-sales-dashboard
Beverage sales dashboard analysis using Power BI to explore revenue, Profit, sales trends, and brand performances.
Justrite Beverages Sales Dashboard

Project Overview

This project analyzes beverage sales data for Justrite to understand business performance using Power BI.

Business Objectives

* Analyze total revenue and profit.
* Identify the best-selling beverage brands by sales volume.
* Compare retailer profitability.
* Examine revenue performance across states and regions.
* Track monthly profit margin trends.

Tools Used

* Microsoft Power BI
* Data visualization
* Business data analysis

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* Total Revenue: $8.2M
* Total Profit: $6.41M
* Total Units Sold: 16M
* Profit Margin: 79%

Key Insights

* Coca-Cola recorded the highest sales volume among the displayed beverage brands.
* The West region generated the highest revenue among the displayed regions.
* Sodapop recorded the highest profit among the displayed retailers.
* Monthly profit margin fluctuated throughout the year.
